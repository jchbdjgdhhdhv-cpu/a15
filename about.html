About

A whole website about one very good coat.

Duffels is an independent guide to the duffel coat.

We write about where the duffel came from, what makes a good one and how to choose, wear and care for it. Our tools let you see how toggles fasten and find a size that leaves room for a jumper.

We do not sell coats, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
