Fit & Care

A duffel should fit over a jumper and last for decades. Here is how to choose the size and keep the wool looking good.

By the Duffels editors · a six minute read

I.Size for your layers

A duffel is worn over knitwear, so measure your chest over the jumper you will wear most, then choose a coat with about 8 to 12 cm of ease. The toggles should fasten without pulling across the chest.

II.Choosing a length

  • Hip: casual and easy for cycling or driving.
  • Thigh: the classic, most versatile length.
  • Knee: the warmest, best over tailoring.

III.Checking the fit

Raise your arms: the coat should not ride up far. Sleeves should cover your shirt cuff with arms by your sides. The hood should sit flat when down, without pulling the collar back.

IV.Caring for heavy wool

Brush the coat after wear with a soft clothes brush to lift dust and restore the nap. Let it dry naturally at room temperature if it gets wet. Spot clean marks gently, and have it professionally cleaned only when really needed.

V.Storing between seasons

Hang the coat on a broad, shaped hanger in a breathable cover. Make sure it is clean before storing, and use cedar or lavender to deter moths. Fasten one toggle so the front hangs straight.
